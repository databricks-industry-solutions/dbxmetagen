Investment operations data notes.

In this schema a "position" is a portfolio holding -- a security a fund owns --
not a job role or a physical location. A "book" is a trading book that groups
related positions. Amounts are in USD unless a currency column indicates
otherwise, and all dates are trade dates in UTC.

Status codes that appear across tables: A = active, C = closed, S = suspended.
Any column ending in _hash (for example ssn_hash) is a salted hash used only for
joins and must never be treated as the real identifier.

When generating descriptions, prefer business terminology over raw column
mechanics -- for example, describe cusip as "the security identifier (CUSIP)"
rather than "a nine-character code."
